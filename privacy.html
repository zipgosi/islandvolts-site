Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Island Volts collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • This website: the site is a set of static pages. It has no sign-up, no forms and no user accounts. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our web host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, under its own privacy policy.
  • When you email us: we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

How we use it

We use the details you send us only to reply, to prepare estimates or provide the service you asked for, and to keep ordinary records. For installation jobs this can include the job address and the contact details of the person who approved the work. Camera footage recorded by a system we install belongs to the customer; we see it only if the customer gives us remote access to help with support.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers we need to do the work (for example, our email, accounting and equipment suppliers), and when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

We keep records as long as needed for the work and for tax and legal reasons. You can ask us to correct or delete your information by email, and we will do so unless we must keep it by law.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
